# Privacy Policy — Community Golf (Android)

**Last updated:** 19 July 2026

This policy describes how Community Golf (“the app”) handles information. Host this document at a public HTTPS URL and paste that URL into the Google Play store listing and Data safety form.

## Who we are

Community Golf is a golf rangefinder app for Android. Contact the publisher listed on the Google Play store listing for privacy requests.

## Information we use

### Location

- The app requests **precise or approximate location** while you use it.
- Location is used **on your device** to estimate distance to the green and tee during a round.
- We do **not** require location to search for courses.
- We do **not** request background location.

### Course and map data

- Course search and scorecards come from **OpenGolfAPI**.
- Hole coordinates come from **OpenStreetMap** (via Overpass), where volunteers have mapped them.
- Map tiles may come from Google Maps (release) or OpenStreetMap / Esri imagery (debug or Maps fallback).

### On-device storage

- The app caches course and hole data on your phone (Room database) so rounds can open faster offline.
- Preferences such as map style are stored on your device.
- Cloud backup of app data is disabled.

### Optional tips

- Optional one-time tips use **Google Play Billing**. Purchase processing is handled by Google; we do not receive your full payment card details.

### Analytics and crash reports

- We use **Firebase Analytics** and **Firebase Crashlytics** to understand feature usage and diagnose crashes.
- Events may include coarse identifiers such as course id and truncated course name, hole numbers, and search result counts — not your name or email (OpenGolf sign-in for contributions is separate from analytics).

## Data sharing

- We do not sell personal information.
- Service providers process data on our behalf (Google Firebase, Google Play, OpenGolfAPI, Overpass/OSM tile providers) under their terms.

## Your choices

- Deny or revoke location permission in Android settings.
- Uninstall the app to remove on-device cache.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use after updates means you accept the revised policy.

## Contact

Use the developer contact email on the Google Play listing.
